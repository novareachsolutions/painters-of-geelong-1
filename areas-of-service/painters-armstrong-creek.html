About painting in Armstrong Creek

Painters you can trust in Armstrong Creek, 3217.

Armstrong Creek is Geelong's biggest growth corridor, with the Warralily, Mirripoa and surrounding estates largely developed since 2010. House painters Armstrong Creek homeowners have trusted since the early stages of these estates have seen the suburb develop from the ground up. Most homes are modern double-storey properties with render-and-brick or full-render exteriors, tiled roofs, composite garage doors and contemporary colour schemes. As residential painters in Armstrong Creek, we've worked on everything from first-paint touch-ups and builder-defect repairs to full repaints on some of the earliest homes, which are now reaching the 10-year mark.

Painting new-estate homes is different work to heritage repaints. The substrates are modern — render, fibre cement, modern brick, builder-grade trim — but they come with their own issues: settling cracks, render imperfections, builder finishes that haven't held up, and the inevitable need to refresh exteriors after the first decade. We use Dulux Weathershield on exteriors, low-VOC Wash&Wear inside, and the prep that gets modern substrates ready for a quality Dulux paint system.

Category 05

Surface Prep & Pressure Cleaning in Armstrong Creek

Pressure washing Armstrong Creek homes is the foundation of every quality repaint. Even brand-new homes benefit from proper prep before the topcoat — render hairline cracks, settling movement, builder-grade finishes that have failed early all need addressing properly. Same crew handles prep through painting.

Specialised prep work

Pressure washing

High-pressure cleaning of render, brick, composite trim, eaves, paths, decks and driveways. Removes lichen, oxidised paint, dust and biological growth before priming — essential even on newer estate homes.

Surface cleaning

Detail cleaning of substrates pressure-washing can't reach — interior plaster, modern cornices, contemporary joinery, timber and metal hardware. Sugar-soap and TSP washes.

Sanding and priming

Hand-sanding, mechanical sanding and full primer systems matched to substrate — oil-based timber primers, masonry sealers, render bonding primers, stain blockers for modern surfaces.

Crack and hole filling

Filling cracks, gaps, splits, hairline render cracks and settling damage in timber, plaster and render — flexible exterior caulks, two-pack fillers, render patch built for modern substrates.

Minor repairs and patching

Replacing damaged timber sections, repairing rotten weatherboard ends, patching render, fixing builder-grade defects before painting. Modern carpentry handled in-house.

Armstrong Creek painting FAQs.

Quick answers to the things Armstrong Creek homeowners ask us most often. Don't see what you need? Just call — we're a quick chat away.

Do you paint new estate homes in Armstrong Creek?

Yes — Armstrong Creek estate homes (Warralily, Mirripoa and surrounds) are a major part of our work. We do first paints, builder-defect repairs, mid-life touch-ups and decade-mark full repaints. Modern render-and-brick substrates handled with Dulux Weathershield systems.

How much does it cost to repaint an Armstrong Creek estate home?

A full exterior repaint of a double-storey render-and-brick estate home in Armstrong Creek typically ranges from $10,000 to $18,000 depending on size, condition, render crack repair required, and trim/Colorbond detail. A full interior repaint of a 4-bedroom home generally falls between $6,000 and $11,000. Free on-site quotes always.

Do you handle builder defect repairs and first-paint touch-ups?

Yes. Builder-defect repairs (render cracks, finish imperfections, builder-grade trim issues) and first-paint refinements are routine Armstrong Creek work. We can handle these as standalone jobs or roll them into a broader repaint scope.

How long does it take to paint a typical Armstrong Creek home?

A full interior repaint of a 4-bedroom estate home usually takes 5-8 working days. A full exterior repaint of a double-storey render-and-brick estate home generally takes 7-12 working days with proper prep.

Do you offer free quotes in Armstrong Creek?

Yes. All quotes in Armstrong Creek are free and provided on-site. We assess condition, render integrity, substrate, scope and access in person, then send a written quote — typically within 24 hours. Call 0431 261 281.
