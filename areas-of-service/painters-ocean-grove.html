About painting in Ocean Grove

Painters you can trust in Ocean Grove, 3226.

Ocean Grove is the Bellarine Peninsula's biggest coastal town — mixed family suburb, retiree haven and holiday destination, with housing that reflects all three. Original 1960s-70s fibro and weatherboard beach shacks sit alongside newer coastal architectural builds, larger family homes inland, and holiday rentals scattered through the back streets. As coastal home painters with 25 years across the Peninsula, we've painted the lot — with Main Beach to 13th Beach as our coastal patch and the inland family streets as our suburban one.

Our repainting services in Ocean Grove are built around one fact: this is coastal painting first. Salt air comes off the Bass Strait, gets pushed inland by the southerlies, and finds every gap in inadequate prep within months. We use marine-grade primer systems, salt-tolerant Dulux Weathershield top-coats, careful timber sealing, and the patience that fibro cladding, exposed timber and metal trim need to last 8-10 years in a coastal environment.

Surface Prep & Pressure Cleaning in Ocean Grove

Pressure washing Ocean Grove homes is non-negotiable before any coastal repaint. Salt removal, careful sanding, marine-grade primer matching, full crack filling — these are the foundation of a coastal repaint that lasts. Pressure cleaning through to minor coastal carpentry all handled by the same crew.

Specialised prep work

Pressure washing

High-pressure cleaning of fibro cladding, fibre cement, timber weatherboards, brick, render, exposed metal, decks and driveways. Removes salt residue, lichen, oxidised paint and biological growth.

Surface cleaning

Detail cleaning of substrates pressure-washing can't reach — interior plaster, cornices, mouldings, salt-affected metal hardware, feature timber. Sugar-soap and TSP washes for salt removal.

Sanding and priming

Hand-sanding, mechanical sanding and full marine-grade primer systems matched to coastal substrate — salt-tolerant timber primers, marine masonry sealers, fibre cement primers, exposed-metal primers.

Crack and hole filling

Filling cracks, gaps, splits and salt-damage in timber, plaster, render and fibre cement — flexible exterior caulks, two-pack fillers and substrate-matched patch built for coastal movement.

Minor repairs and patching

Replacing salt-damaged timber sections, repairing rotten weatherboard or fascia ends, patching fibro and fibre cement, re-securing loose trims. Coastal carpentry in-house.

FAQ

Ocean Grove painting FAQs.

Quick answers to the things Ocean Grove homeowners ask us most often. Don't see what you need? Just call — we're a quick chat away.

Do you use marine-grade systems for Ocean Grove coastal painting?

Yes — marine-grade primer systems and salt-tolerant Dulux Weathershield top-coats are default on Ocean Grove coastal jobs, not an upgrade. We treat fibro cladding, fibre cement, exposed metal trim and beachfront timber with substrate-matched systems built for Bellarine salt-air exposure.

How much does it cost to repaint an Ocean Grove beach home?

A full exterior repaint of a coastal home in Ocean Grove typically ranges from $9,000 to $20,000 depending on size, salt exposure level, prep required, and substrate mix (fibro, fibre cement, weatherboard, brick). Beachfront properties cost more because they need heavier marine-grade systems. Free on-site quotes always.

Do you service all of Ocean Grove including 13th Beach and inland streets?

Yes. We cover all of Ocean Grove 3226 — from Main Beach and The Terrace through to 13th Beach, Wallington Road, and the inland family streets. About 30 minutes from our Geelong base.

Can you repaint fibro cladding common in older Ocean Grove beach houses?

Yes — fibro and fibre cement cladding repaints are a routine Ocean Grove job. We use fibre cement-specific primers, careful prep to avoid disturbing any asbestos-containing material (where present, we handle it safely and to regulation), and salt-tolerant top-coat systems built for the substrate.

Do you offer free quotes in Ocean Grove?

Yes. All quotes in Ocean Grove are free and provided on-site. We assess condition, substrate, salt exposure level, scope and access in person, then send a written quote — typically within 24 hours. Call 0431 261 281.

How much do house painters charge in Ocean Grove?

House painting costs in Ocean Grove vary by scope and salt exposure. Single-room interior repaints typically start around $600–$1,200. Whole-home interior repaints for a 3–4 bedroom range $5,000–$11,000. Exterior repaints on coastal homes run $9,000–$20,000+ depending on substrate, prep and proximity to the beach. All quotes are free and on-site — request one here.

What paint works best for coastal homes in Ocean Grove?

For Ocean Grove coastal homes we use Dulux Weathershield as the standard exterior top-coat, paired with marine-grade primers matched to substrate — timber primers for weatherboard, fibre cement primers for fibro and Hardie cladding, masonry sealers for render and brick, and exposed-metal primers for Colorbond and trim. Salt-tolerant acrylic systems consistently outperform standard exterior paints by 3–5 years in beachfront exposure. UV-stable timber sealers are recommended on exposed decking and feature timber.

How often should coastal homes be repainted?

Coastal homes need repainting more often than inland because of salt-air exposure. As a rule:

  • Beachfront exteriors (within 2 streets of the water): every 5–7 years
  • Mid-Ocean Grove exteriors: every 7–9 years
  • Inland family homes: every 8–12 years
  • Decks & exposed timber: fresh seal every 2–3 years

A proper marine-grade paint system with thorough prep at the start can extend these intervals significantly.
