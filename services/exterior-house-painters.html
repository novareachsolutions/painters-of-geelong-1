Paint Systems

Paint Systems & Surface Preparation

The right paint system depends on more than the surface itself. We factor in the substrate, whether previous coatings have failed or are still sound, how much UV and coastal exposure the wall gets, moisture history, and the finish you're after — a satin acrylic behaves differently to a low-sheen on a sun-exposed north wall. Before quoting, we check for failed coatings, moisture damage, and (on timber) how much the boards have moved seasonally, which determines whether a section needs a light sand or a full strip-back. Paint systems from Dulux, Haymes and Wattyl are then selected against those factors rather than one default product across every job.

Every recommendation is based on the condition of the existing coating, the substrate, local weather exposure and the expected lifespan of the finish — not the same specification applied to every home.

FAQ

Exterior Painting FAQs

How much does exterior painting cost in Geelong?

Most single-storey exterior repaints in Geelong cost between $7,000 and $14,000, depending on the home's size, condition and surfaces.

How long does exterior painting take?

Most exterior house painting projects take 5 to 9 working days, depending on the home's size, weather and preparation required.

How often should a home exterior be repainted?

Most homes need repainting every 7 to 10 years. Coastal properties often require repainting sooner because of salt air and stronger weather exposure.

Do you provide free exterior painting quotes?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation, written quotes after inspecting your property.

What preparation is included?

Preparation includes pressure washing, scraping, sanding, filling gaps, repairing minor defects and priming bare surfaces before painting.

Can you paint in wet weather?

No. We only paint in suitable weather conditions to ensure proper adhesion, curing and long-lasting results.

What paint products do you use?

We use premium exterior paint systems selected for Geelong's climate and matched to the surface being painted.

Do you paint brick and weatherboard homes?

Yes. We paint weatherboard, brick, rendered homes, fences, garage doors and exterior trims.

What areas do you service?

We service Geelong and surrounding suburbs including Belmont, Highton, Newtown, Grovedale, Armstrong Creek, Torquay and Ocean Grove.

How long will exterior paint last?

A professionally applied exterior paint system typically lasts 7 to 10 years, depending on the surface and weather exposure.

Do I need to be home while the work is completed?

No. You only need to provide access to the property and be available for the initial and final walkthrough if required.

Can previously painted brick be repainted?

Yes. Previously painted brick can be repainted once the existing coating is cleaned, sound and properly prepared.

Can you paint a Colorbond fence?

Yes. Colorbond fences can be repainted using the correct preparation methods and metal coating system.

Do you repair damaged weatherboards before painting?

Yes. Minor weatherboard repairs are completed before priming and painting to provide a sound surface.
