Wall Painting

Complete wall painting in Geelong homes — living rooms, bedrooms, hallways and kitchens. Walls are washed, filled and sanded before two full coats of washable low-sheen acrylic go on, with guidance on the right interior wall paint and sheen included so high-traffic walls end up with a finish that wipes clean instead of marking.

Repainting interior walls over dark or dated colours is where preparation pays off — a tinted undercoat and a quality trade acrylic from Dulux, Haymes or Wattyl covers in two coats instead of four, which is the difference between a one-day room and a three-day one.

Ceiling Painting

Ceiling painting for living areas, kitchens and bathrooms — from targeted touch-ups after repairs to complete ceiling repainting in dedicated flat ceiling white. Our ceiling painters treat stains, cracks and mould before painting — not covered over — so the ceiling stays bright instead of the mark bleeding back through.

Ceiling painting Geelong jobs are quoted on height, condition and repairs — water stains need a stain-blocking primer first or they bleed straight through fresh ceiling white, and bathrooms need a mould-resistant formulation rather than standard flat paint.

Door & Trim Painting

Skirting boards, architraves and door frames painted in hard-wearing water-based enamel, plus complete door painting for Geelong homes. Trims are sanded between every coat and finished last — after ceilings and walls — which is how the lines stay crisp and the surface ends up smooth rather than showing brush marks.

Skirting board painting and architrave work is finished in water-based enamel, sanded between every coat — the modern choice that stays white for years instead of yellowing the way old oil-based enamels do.

Feature wall painting Geelong homes ask for most is the bedhead wall or the living room focal wall — the wall the room naturally faces. Get the placement right and one wall changes the whole room; get it wrong and it reads as an unfinished paint job.

Paint Systems

Paint Systems & Surface Preparation

The right paint system depends on more than the surface itself. We factor in the room, how it's used, whether previous coatings are sound, and the finish you're after — a hallway wall that gets brushed past daily needs a washable low-sheen acrylic, while a bedroom ceiling needs a dedicated flat that hides every imperfection. Before quoting, we check for cracks, old patch repairs, water stains and drummy plaster, which determines whether a wall needs a light sand or full repair work first. Paint systems from Dulux, Haymes and Wattyl — including low VOC and washable paint options — are then selected against those factors rather than one default product across every job.

Every recommendation is based on the condition of the existing surface, the room's use, and the expected lifespan of the finish — not the same specification applied to every home.

FAQ

Interior Painting FAQs

How much does interior house painting cost in Geelong?

Most full interior repaints in Geelong cost between $5,000 and $9,500 for a standard three-bedroom home, with single rooms from around $400 and apartments from $2,500. Surface condition, ceiling height and colour changes move the price — our interior painting cost guide breaks down every rate, and we provide a free, itemised quote after inspecting your home.

How long does an interior painting job take?

Most standard interior painting jobs take 2–5 days depending on the size of the property and the scope of work. We provide a timeline estimate with every quote.

What areas do you cover for interior painting?

We provide interior painting services across Geelong, including Newtown, Belmont, Grovedale, Highton, Waurn Ponds, Lara, Torquay, Armstrong Creek, and Ocean Grove.

What types of paint do you use?

We use high-quality, non-toxic paints from premium brands like Dulux, Haymes and Wattyl, designed for Australian conditions, including low VOC and washable paint options. We offer a range of finishes including matte, low sheen, satin, semi-gloss, and gloss.

Do you offer colour consultation?

Yes — colour consultation is included with every quote. We advise based on the room's light, how it's used and your existing fixtures, and we recommend test patches on your actual walls before committing, because colours read differently under Geelong's southern light than they do on a swatch.

Do I need to move furniture before painting?

No — moving and protecting furniture is part of every job. Floors, furniture and fixtures are covered with drop sheets and plastic before any sanding starts, and everything goes back where it was at handover.

Can you use exterior paint for interior walls?

No — exterior paints contain additives designed for UV and weather resistance that release higher VOCs indoors and cure differently on interior surfaces. Interior walls need purpose-made low VOC acrylics, which are safer, lower-odour and formulated for washability inside the home.

What is the best interior paint to use?

For walls, a washable low sheen acrylic from a trade line such as Dulux, Haymes or Wattyl gives the best balance of durability and finish; ceilings need a dedicated flat ceiling white, and trims a water-based enamel in satin or semi-gloss. Our wall painting guide explains how to choose.

Do you paint walls or ceilings first?

Ceilings are always painted first, then walls, then trims and doors last. Working top-down means any splatter lands on surfaces that haven't been finished yet — it's how professionals avoid rework, and it's covered step by step in our interior house painting guide.

Do you repaint rental and end-of-lease properties?

Yes — end of lease painting and rental turnarounds are a specialty. Vacant properties are typically completed in 1–3 days, keeping vacancy time to a minimum. See our apartment painting guide for how rental repaints are scoped and priced.

How much does it cost to paint a 2-bedroom apartment?

A full interior repaint of a 2-bedroom apartment in Geelong typically costs $3,500–$5,500, depending on condition and colour changes. A 1-bedroom unit runs $2,500–$4,000, and we manage body corporate access requirements as part of the job.

When is the best time to paint interiors?

Interior painting can be done year-round. Autumn and winter often have better availability and pricing, while spring and summer book out early — modern low-odour acrylics dry well indoors in any season with normal ventilation.

Do you supply the paint?

Yes — paint is included in our quotes, using trade lines from Dulux, Haymes and Wattyl. Your quote names the specific product and sheen for each surface, so you know exactly what's going on your walls before work starts.

Do you repair walls and ceilings before painting?

Yes — crack repairs, patch repairs, filling holes, sanding and priming are part of our preparation process. Any larger plaster repairs are scoped in your quote upfront, rather than discovered partway through the job.
