Surface Prep & Pressure
Cleaning in Geelong

Great paint results start with great preparation. Our professional surface prep and pressure cleaning services ensure your surfaces are perfectly clean, smooth, and ready for a flawless, long-lasting paint finish.

Professional Power Washing Services

We provide professional high pressure cleaning services in Geelong for residential and commercial properties. Our team thoroughly cleans timber, concrete, and other outdoor surfaces, removing dirt, grime, mould, algae, and weathered buildup. Using specialised power washing services, we restore surfaces to their best condition, ensuring a smooth, clean base ready for any finishing or maintenance work while extending the life of your surfaces.

In addition to general surface prep, we specialise in preparing areas for deck staining services and work with the best fence painters to ensure every surface is properly cleaned, repaired, and ready for a flawless finish. Our careful preparation helps coatings adhere better, last longer, and maintain their appearance, giving you durable and high-quality results every time.

Pressure Washing

Clear away dirt, mould, grime, and old paint using reliable professional pressure washing services. As one of the best pressure wash cleaners in Geelong, we restore driveways, walls, decks, and all exterior surfaces to a clean, refreshed condition ready for painting or sealing.

Surface Cleaning

We provide thorough surface cleaning services to remove contaminants before painting. By using advanced power washer surface cleaner techniques, we thoroughly remove dust, grease, and debris from hardwood, concrete, and other surfaces, creating a clean base for optimal paint adhesion and a durable, long-lasting finish.

Sanding & Priming

Professional sanding and priming to create the perfect base for painting. Our sanding services cover all timber surfaces, while our floor sanding and polishing services ensure hardwood and timber floors are smooth, sealed, and ready for the right primer for a durable, long-lasting finish.

Crack & Hole Filling

Expert repair for cracks and holes in walls, ceilings, and exterior surfaces. We carefully carry out wall crack repair and foundation crack repair, smoothing and reinforcing areas affected by cracks in walls or any surface that needs to fix foundation crack, leaving everything properly prepared for painting.

Minor Repairs & Patching

Small repairs that make a big difference. We repair damaged plaster, rotted timber, loose render, and other surface imperfections, delivering expert minor repairs and patching services to leave every surface smooth, solid, and ready for painting.

Mould & Stain Treatment

Remove mould, water stains, and surface discolouration before repainting with reliable mould treatment services. Our professional mould cleaners use targeted treatments to stop regrowth and prevent bleed-through, giving you a clean, lasting result backed by an experienced mould removal specialist.

FAQ

FAQ-Surface Prep and Cleaning Services

Why is surface preparation important before painting?

Proper surface preparation ensures paint adheres correctly, looks smooth, and lasts longer. Skipping prep leads to peeling, bubbling, and premature paint failure.

Do you offer pressure cleaning as a standalone service?

Yes, we offer pressure cleaning independently of painting. It's great for driveways, patios, decks, fences, and building exteriors.

How do you treat mould before painting?

We apply professional-grade mould treatment solutions, allow them to work, then clean and prime the surface with mould-resistant primer before painting.

Can you repair damaged walls before painting?

Absolutely. We handle crack filling, plaster patching, sanding, and all necessary repairs to ensure a smooth, even surface ready for painting.

How long does surface preparation take?

Preparation time depends on the condition of the surfaces. Minor prep may take a few hours, while extensive repairs and cleaning can take 1–2 days. We'll include a timeline in your quote.
