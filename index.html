Are you a local painting company near me?

Yes. Painters of Geelong is a local, owner-operated painting company providing residential and commercial painting services throughout Geelong and surrounding suburbs, including Belmont, Highton, Newtown, Grovedale, Torquay, Ocean Grove, Lara, Waurn Ponds and Armstrong Creek. If you're searching for painters near you in the Geelong region, we're here to help with a free, no-obligation quote.

Do you offer free house painting quotes in Geelong?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotes for all residential and commercial painting projects across Geelong. Every quote is itemised, transparent and tailored to your property's needs, so you know exactly what's included before any work begins.

What painting services do you offer in Geelong?

We provide a complete range of residential and commercial painting services across Geelong, including interior house painting, exterior house painting, commercial painting, deck and timber staining, fence painting, and surface preparation and pressure cleaning.

Yes, we're fully insured with public liability cover on every job. Painting doesn't require a trade licence in Victoria.

House painting costs vary depending on the size of your home, the condition of the surfaces, the type of paint selected and the scope of work. We provide a free, itemised quote for every job so you know exactly what to expect before committing — contact us today for a fast estimate.
