Geelong Climate

What Geelong's Climate Means for Your Paint Job

If you've ever wondered why one Geelong home looks freshly painted ten years on while another is peeling after three, the answer comes back to two things — the products specified, and how the surface was prepared.

Our house painters in Geelong work on everything from heritage weatherboards to modern coastal homes. Geelong sits in a unique pocket of climate. Sea air off Corio Bay and Bass Strait carries salt that breaks down standard acrylic paints from the outside in. Long summer UV fades dark colours within 3–5 years. And the 20°C daily swings stress every paint film on the substrate underneath.

This is why our quotes specify products by name — Dulux Weathershield, Haymes Solashield, Wattyl Solagard — and why we won't cut a price by skipping prep. In Geelong, prep is the paint job. Browse our services below.

FAQ

Painting in Geelong — Frequently Asked Questions

How much do painters cost in Geelong?

Most painters in Geelong quote by the square metre. Standard 2026 rates are $25–$40 per m² for interior walls, $30–$55 per m² for exterior walls, and $35–$60 per m² for commercial work. Hourly rates sit between $40 and $70 per hour.

How long does it take to paint a house in Geelong?

A standard three-bedroom interior repaint takes 4–7 working days. A full exterior repaint takes 7–14 days depending on weather, prep and surface area. Commercial projects are scheduled around your operating hours with a guaranteed completion date.

Do you supply the paint or do I?

We supply all paint, primers, sealers and materials as part of every quote — you don't source anything. We pass on our trade pricing and only specify premium product lines from Dulux, Taubmans, Haymes, Wattyl and Resene.

What paint brands do you use?

We specify Dulux, Taubmans, Haymes, Wattyl, Resene, Intergrain and Cabot's, matching the product to the surface. Dulux Weathershield or Haymes Solashield for exteriors, Wash&Wear for high-traffic interiors, Intergrain for decks, and low-VOC ranges for families and rentals.

What's the best time of year to paint a house in Geelong?

For exteriors, late spring (October–November) and autumn (March–May) are ideal — mild temperatures, low rainfall and stable humidity for proper curing. Interior painting can be done year-round.

Can you paint in winter?

Yes — interiors are unaffected by winter. Exteriors are possible on dry days with overnight temperatures above 10°C, but we typically schedule major exterior projects between September and May for optimal cure conditions.

Do you do small jobs like a single room or feature wall?

Absolutely. We do single-room repaints, feature walls, ceiling-only jobs, trim refreshes and small touch-ups. No job is too small.

Do you do both residential and commercial painting?

Yes — we service homeowners, landlords, real estate agencies, body corporates, offices, retail shopfronts, warehouses and industrial sites throughout Geelong.

How do I prepare my home before painters arrive?

Move smaller furniture and breakables out of the work area, take down wall hangings and curtains, and clear access to the rooms or surfaces being painted. We handle all drop sheets, masking and floor protection.

Do you handle lead paint testing for older homes in Geelong?

Yes. For homes built before 1970 we test for lead paint before any sanding or scraping begins, and follow EPA Victoria guidelines for safe removal and disposal where lead is present.
